What we do

Every job is some mix of these three.

Clear-outs

We bag up the trash and junk and load it onto our truck, so you don’t have to lift anything.

Organizing

We go through what’s left with you and put it back in an order that makes sense, using the shelves and space you already have.

Haul-away

Old furniture, boxes, things you forgot you had. If you don’t want it anymore, it leaves with us.

About us

I’m Spencer Dilworth, an 18-year-old freshman in college. My brother is in high school. We’re building Franklin Garage Cleaners together, one garage at a time.

Growing up in a big family, our garage was always packed with things we didn’t use anymore, had forgotten about, or never got around to throwing out. When I talked to other people, I kept hearing the same thing. Some don’t have kids at home to help, some don’t have the time, and some would just rather not take on the whole garage themselves. So my brother and I decided to start a garage cleaning business together. We’re just getting started and looking for our first clients, so if your garage could use some help, we’d love to hear from you.
